Meet Lilya

Every cake starts with a conversation: your story, your colours, your flavours. Then I make something that exists only once.

Lilya

Passion Gourmande is the cake studio of Lilya, a cake artist based in Ottawa. Her work brings together French pâtisserie and the flavours of family celebrations, from wedding cakes to henna-night buffets.

No two orders are the same. Tell her about your event, share a few inspiration photos, and she will design a cake that looks like you and tastes even better.

How it works

Ordering, step by step

  1. Tell me about your event Fill in the order form or send a message on Instagram: date, number of guests, ideas.
  2. Design it together We choose the design, flavours and size. Inspiration photos are welcome.
  3. Confirm your order You receive a personalised quote. Your order is confirmed once you agree.
  4. Celebrate Pick up your creation and enjoy the moment with your guests.

Plan ahead: dates fill up quickly, especially for weddings and holidays. The earlier you write, the better.
